# Child Care and Vaccination Guidance

## Well-child visits

Regular well-child visits track growth and development. During these visits the doctor measures height and weight, reviews feeding and sleep, and discusses milestones. Bring your child's vaccination card and growth chart to every appointment. If your child is unwell, mention the symptoms, their duration, and any medicines already given.

## Vaccination schedule

Vaccinations protect children from serious diseases. The schedule depends on age and on which doses have already been given. The pediatrician will review your child's record and suggest the appropriate timing. Mild fever or soreness after a vaccination is common; however, persistent high fever, difficulty breathing, or unusual drowsiness needs urgent attention. Call the clinic during working hours, or 112 in an emergency.

## Common childhood concerns

Fever, cough, runny nose, loose motions and poor appetite are common. Keep the child comfortable and offer fluids. Do not give medicines without advice, and never guess a dose based on an adult medicine. The doctor will examine the child and explain the appropriate care. Avoid self-medication, especially with antibiotics.

## Preparing for the appointment

Bring a change of clothes, a favourite toy, and a small snack for after the visit. Try to schedule the appointment around the child's nap time so the child is calm. Tell the reception if your child has any allergies, particularly to medicines or food. The consultation fee for Dr. Shah, our pediatrician, is six hundred rupees.

The pediatrician would rather review a worried parent's concern than miss an early sign of illness. Keep a written list of questions so you do not forget them during the consultation.
