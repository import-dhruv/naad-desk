# Procedure Preparation Instructions

## General guidance

Before any procedure, the doctor will explain what will happen, how long it takes, and what you should do afterwards. Please arrive on time, carry your identification and previous reports, and tell the reception staff if you are taking any medicines, especially blood thinners. You may ask questions at any point; the clinical team will answer them before you give consent.

## Blood tests

Some blood tests require fasting for eight to ten hours. Water is generally allowed, but you should avoid tea, coffee and juice. If you are diabetic or elderly, speak to the doctor before fasting, because skipping food may not be safe for you. Wear loose clothing so the arm can be exposed easily. After the sample is taken, a small plaster is applied; keep the area clean and avoid heavy activity for a short while. Results are usually available the same afternoon or the next morning.

## Skin procedures

On the morning of a skin procedure, do not apply new creams, oils or cosmetics to the affected area. Come with clean skin. If a biopsy or minor surgery is planned, the doctor will explain the local anaesthetic and the aftercare. Keep the area dry for the advised period and return for review as scheduled. Do not scratch or pick at the site, even if it itches.

## Imaging and X-rays

For X-rays, remove jewellery and metal objects from the area being examined. You may be asked to change into a gown. Tell the technician if you might be pregnant. For scans that require a full bladder, drink water as instructed and avoid emptying your bladder until after the test. The radiologist will send a report to the consulting doctor, who will discuss the findings with you.

## Follow-up after procedures

Most procedures are done on an outpatient basis and you can go home the same day. You may experience mild discomfort, which the doctor will explain how to manage. Contact the clinic if you notice persistent bleeding, fever, or increasing pain. For urgent concerns outside clinic hours, call the emergency number 112. The reception can arrange a follow-up appointment with the same doctor.
