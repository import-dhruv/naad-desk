# Detailed Appointment Guide

## The six steps

### Step 1: Choose a doctor
Decide which department fits your concern. The general physician handles everyday ailments, the dermatologist handles skin and hair, the pediatrician handles children, and the orthopedist handles bones and joints. The reception can guide you if you are unsure.

### Step 2: Choose a date and time
Appointments are available Monday to Saturday in thirty-minute slots. You may ask for a specific day, a relative day such as tomorrow, or a part of the day such as morning. If your request is ambiguous, the assistant will ask a clarifying question rather than make an assumption.

### Step 3: Provide your details
Give your full name and a ten-digit phone number. The number is used to verify your identity if you later reschedule or cancel. In dashboards and logs it is masked so only the last four digits appear.

### Step 4: Confirm the read-back
The assistant repeats the doctor, date, time, patient name and masked phone number. You must say a clear yes before anything is saved. No booking is created before this step.

### Step 5: Receive confirmation
Once confirmed, the exact details are spoken again and an appointment number is provided. Keep this number for future reference. A reminder is sent the previous evening.

### Step 6: Attend the visit
Arrive ten minutes early with your identification and reports. If you are delayed by more than fifteen minutes, the reception may offer the next free slot.

## Common question 1
Q: Can I book for someone else? A: Yes. Provide the patient's name and a contact number. The patient should carry identification when they arrive. Q: What if I need to change the time? A: Call at least four hours in advance and quote the phone number. The old slot is released only after the new one is confirmed. Q: Can I book two appointments? A: Yes, subject to availability, but please choose different slots.
