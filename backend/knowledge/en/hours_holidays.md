# Working Hours and Holidays

## Regular hours

Sharma Clinic operates Monday through Saturday. The morning session is from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and the afternoon session is from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays. The telephone line is answered during working hours; calls received after hours are recorded and returned the next working day.

## Slot timings

Appointments are scheduled in 30-minute slots. The available start times are 09:00, 09:30, 10:00, 10:30, 11:00, 11:30, 14:00, 14:30, 15:00, 15:30, 16:00, 16:30. Please arrive a few minutes before your slot. If you are more than fifteen minutes late, the reception may offer the next available slot instead. Emergency cases are always prioritised.

## Holidays

The clinic remains closed on national and regional holidays, on the second Saturday of every month, and on days declared by the clinic management. A holiday notice is displayed at the entrance and on the website at least one week in advance. If your appointment falls on a holiday, the reception will proactively contact you to reschedule. The voice assistant consults the holiday calendar before offering dates and will never suggest a closed day.

## Seasonal variations

During festival weeks the clinic may run a shortened morning session. Any change to regular hours is announced in advance. When in doubt, please call the reception desk to confirm that the doctor is available on a particular day.
