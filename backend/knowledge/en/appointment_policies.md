# Appointment Policies

## Booking

An appointment is created only after the patient or caller explicitly confirms the details read back by the receptionist or the voice assistant. A booking is never created on the basis of an assumption. The caller must provide a name and a contact number; the contact number is used to verify identity for later changes and is stored only in masked form in dashboards.

## Confirmation and reminders

After a booking is committed, a confirmation number is provided. A reminder message is sent to the registered number on the previous evening. Please keep the confirmation number handy if you need to reschedule or cancel. The voice assistant quotes the exact date and time from the appointment database.

## Rescheduling

You may reschedule free of charge up to four hours before the appointment. Quote the phone number used at the time of booking so the reception can verify identity. The old slot is released only after the new slot is secured, and both changes are applied together. Rescheduling to a different doctor is possible subject to availability.

## Cancellation and no-shows

Cancellations made at least four hours in advance carry no fee. Frequent no-shows may require advance payment for future appointments. If you cannot attend, please cancel early so another patient can use the slot. The voice assistant can cancel an appointment after verifying your phone number and reading back the appointment details.

## Walk-ins

Walk-in patients are seen when a slot is free, but booked appointments take priority. Waiting time for a walk-in depends on the clinic's workload on that day.
