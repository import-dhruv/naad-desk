# Insurance and Reimbursement Details

## How insurance works at the clinic

Sharma Clinic works with a limited panel of insurance providers. Insurance is a payment arrangement; it does not change the clinical decisions made by your doctor. At the time of registration, present your insurance card and a government photo ID. The reception will verify your policy and let you know whether cashless treatment is possible for your visit.

## Cashless treatment

Cashless treatment means the insurer pays the clinic directly and you do not pay at the counter. Approval depends on your policy terms, the treatment proposed, and the insurer's response. The reception cannot guarantee approval and will never quote a coverage amount. If approval is delayed, you may choose to pay directly and claim reimbursement later.

## Reimbursement

For reimbursement, pay at the counter and collect a detailed receipt and a treatment summary. Submit these documents to your insurer along with the claim form. Most insurers require the receipt within a specified period, commonly thirty days. Processing time varies by insurer. The clinic can provide supporting documents but cannot intervene in the insurer's decision.

## What is not covered

Routine check-ups, cosmetic procedures and certain medicines may be excluded from your policy. Consultation fees are generally payable directly unless your plan specifically includes outpatient coverage. Please read your policy document or contact your insurer for exact details. The clinic reception is happy to explain what documents are needed but cannot interpret policy wording.

## Transparency

Every charge is displayed and explained before payment. The voice assistant reads exact fees from the clinic database and never invents an amount. If you are unsure about a figure, ask the receptionist to repeat it and confirm it against your receipt. All financial information in this demonstration is synthetic.
