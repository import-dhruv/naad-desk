# Frequently Asked Questions

## What are the clinic hours?
The clinic is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The morning session runs from 9:00 to 12:00 and the afternoon session from 2:00 to 5:00 PM.

## Is the clinic open on Sunday?
No. The clinic remains closed on Sundays and on declared holidays. The holiday calendar is published on the notice board and on the website.

## How do I book an appointment?
You may call the reception desk, use the website form, or ask this voice assistant to find and confirm a slot. A booking is confirmed only after you verbally approve the read-back.

## Can I reschedule my appointment?
Yes. Please call at least four hours before the scheduled time and quote the phone number used at the time of booking. Rescheduling is free of charge.

## What is the cancellation policy?
Appointments may be cancelled up to four hours before the visit without a fee. Repeated no-shows may require advance payment for future bookings.

## Which payment methods are accepted?
Cash, UPI, credit and debit cards, and net banking are accepted at the reception. UPI is the preferred method.

## Do you accept insurance?
We accept a limited panel of insurers. Please carry your insurance card and a government photo ID. Cashless treatment is subject to insurer approval.

## What documents should I bring?
Please bring a government photo ID, any previous prescriptions, relevant lab reports, and your insurance card if applicable. For children, carry the vaccination record.

## Where is the clinic located?
The clinic is in the heart of the city near the central bus stand, with clear signage and wheelchair access. Parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Do I need to fast before a blood test?
For fasting blood tests, avoid food for eight to ten hours. Water is allowed. Confirm the specific requirement when you book.

## Can I get a medical certificate?
Certificates are issued after a consultation by the treating doctor, subject to the clinic's documentation policy.

## Is there a fee for a follow-up visit?
A standard consultation fee applies to each visit unless included in a follow-up package. The reception can confirm the exact amount.
