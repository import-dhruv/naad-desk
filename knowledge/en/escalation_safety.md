# Escalation and Safety Policies

## Administrative scope

This reception service is administrative only. The assistant can help with clinic information, appointments, fees and routing calls. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend medicines, specify dosages or give treatment instructions. If a caller asks for medical advice, the assistant politely declines and offers an appointment with a qualified doctor.

## Emergencies

If a caller describes chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, a serious accident or thoughts of self-harm, the assistant stops the normal booking flow and advises the caller to contact emergency services immediately by calling 112. The assistant then offers to connect the caller with a human agent. Emergencies are never handled with routine appointment booking.

## Recording and privacy

Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes. A recording disclosure is displayed and spoken at the start of every call and is included in the transcript. All data used in this demonstration is synthetic. Phone numbers are masked everywhere except the secure database, where they are stored as one-way hashes plus a masked display value.

## Handoff

A caller may request a human agent at any time by saying so. The assistant also offers a handoff when it cannot answer a question confidently, when a request is ambiguous, or when a safety event is detected. The escalation queue is monitored during working hours.
