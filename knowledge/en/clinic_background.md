# About Sharma Clinic — Background

## Our story

Sharma Clinic began as a small single-doctor practice and grew into a multi-speciality clinic serving several neighbourhoods. The clinic's philosophy is simple: listen carefully, explain clearly, and treat every patient with dignity. Although the clinic in this project is entirely synthetic, it reflects the way a modern outpatient clinic organises its work.

## How the day runs

The day begins with the morning session at nine. The reception team opens the register, confirms the day's appointments and checks the holiday calendar. Doctors review their lists and any pending reports. Patients arrive, register at the desk, and are called into the consulting room in order. The afternoon session begins at two and continues until five. At the end of the day, the team reviews the next day's schedule and prepares reminder messages.

## Technology at the desk

Appointments are stored in a simple database with doctors, slots and patients. Each slot can hold only one confirmed appointment, which prevents double booking. Changes are applied in a single transaction so that releasing one slot and occupying another always happen together. The voice assistant accesses the same database and reads figures directly from it, so spoken information matches what is on screen.

## Continuous improvement

Calls are reviewed for quality. Transcripts and summaries help the team understand common questions and improve the knowledge base. Experiments measure speech recognition accuracy across languages, latency, and behaviour under degraded audio. Findings are recorded with evidence so that improvements are traceable. All measurement data in this demonstration is generated within the project.

A good reception experience saves time for both patients and doctors. Clear policies, accurate figures and a calm voice build trust. When the system does not know an answer, saying so and offering a human handoff is better than guessing. These principles guide every interaction at the clinic.
