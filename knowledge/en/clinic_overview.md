# Sharma Clinic — Overview

## About the clinic

Sharma Clinic is a neighbourhood multi-speciality outpatient clinic serving families in the city. The clinic focuses on primary care, dermatology, pediatrics and orthopedics. Every consultation is conducted by a qualified doctor, and the reception team supports patients in Hindi, Gujarati and English. The clinic uses synthetic data for demonstrations; no real patient records are stored here.

## Departments

The general physician handles common ailments such as fever, cough, cold, blood pressure monitoring and diabetes follow-up. The dermatology department treats acne, rashes, eczema, hair fall and skin allergies. The pediatrics department looks after child health, vaccination schedules and nutrition. The orthopedics department manages joint pain, sprains, fractures and back pain.

## Getting started

New patients are requested to arrive ten minutes early to complete registration. Please carry a government photo identification document and any previous medical records. The reception desk can answer questions about fees, timings, parking and the documents required for your visit.
