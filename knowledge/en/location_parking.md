# Location and Parking

## Finding the clinic

Sharma Clinic is located in the city centre near the central bus stand, on the main road opposite the municipal park. The building has a distinctive blue signboard and is visible from the bus stop. Auto-rickshaws and taxis are readily available. The nearest metro station is a ten-minute walk away.

## Accessibility

The clinic is on the ground floor and is wheelchair accessible. A ramp is available at the main entrance, and the reception desk is at wheelchair height. Visitors needing assistance may request a volunteer at the entrance.

## Parking

Limited on-site parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis. Two-wheelers may be parked in the marked area to the left of the building. Four-wheelers can use the paid parking lot two minutes away. During peak morning hours the on-site lot fills quickly, so patients are encouraged to arrive early or use public transport.

## Landmarks

Nearby landmarks include the central library, the post office and the municipal park. If you are having trouble finding the clinic, call the reception desk and the team will guide you.
