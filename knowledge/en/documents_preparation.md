# Documents Required Before Appointments

## For all visits

Please carry a government-issued photo identification document such as an Aadhaar card, driving licence or passport. Bring any previous prescriptions, lab reports and scans that are relevant to your visit. A list of current medicines, including dosage, helps the doctor review your treatment safely.

## For specific departments

For dermatology visits, bring photographs of the affected area if the condition varies over time, and a list of skincare products currently in use. For pediatric visits, bring the child's vaccination record and growth chart. For orthopedics, bring earlier X-rays and MRI reports. For general physician visits, bring recent blood test results and blood pressure readings if available.

## Insurance and payment documents

If you wish to use insurance, carry your insurance card and a government photo ID. For cashless treatment, the insurer may also require a referral letter. A copy of the appointment confirmation is helpful at the reception desk.

## Preparation notes

For fasting blood tests, avoid food for eight to ten hours; water is permitted. For skin procedures, avoid applying new creams on the morning of the visit. If your appointment involves a minor procedure, the doctor will explain the preparation during the consultation. Never stop prescribed medication without consulting your doctor first.
