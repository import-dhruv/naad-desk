# Dermatology and Orthopedics Notes

## Skin health

Skin conditions are often chronic and need consistent care. Acne, eczema, pigmentation and hair fall improve with a combination of medication and lifestyle adjustments. Avoid harsh soaps, scrubbing and unverified home remedies. Sunscreen is recommended when advised by the doctor. Photographs taken over several weeks help the doctor judge progress, because changes can be slow.

## When to seek care for skin problems

Seek care if a rash spreads quickly, blisters form, there is severe itching, or the skin becomes painful and warm. Skin infections can worsen rapidly in people with diabetes. The dermatologist may prescribe a short course of medicine and ask you to return for review. Never share medicines with another person, even if the symptoms look similar.

## Bone and joint health

Joint pain, back pain and sprains are common. Rest, ice and elevation may help immediately after an injury, but persistent pain needs assessment. The orthopedist may recommend exercises, physiotherapy, or imaging. Fractures require prompt attention; do not attempt to move an injured person unnecessarily if a fracture is suspected.

## Recovery and exercise

Recovery from sprains and fractures takes time. Follow the doctor's advice about weight bearing and physiotherapy. Returning to activity too early can cause re-injury. Maintain adequate calcium and vitamin D through diet as advised. Older adults should take care to prevent falls at home.
