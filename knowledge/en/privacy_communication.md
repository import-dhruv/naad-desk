# Communication, Privacy and Recording

## Languages

The reception team and the voice assistant can communicate in Hindi, Gujarati and English, including conversational mixtures such as Hinglish. You may begin a conversation in one language and switch to another; the assistant will follow your lead. If a word is misunderstood, please repeat it slowly or spell it out. Names and phone numbers are always read back for confirmation.

## Recording disclosure

Calls may be recorded for quality assurance and training. A disclosure is shown on screen and spoken aloud at the beginning of every call, and the notice is added to the transcript. If you do not wish to be recorded, you may say so and the agent will arrange a human callback. All recordings are handled according to the clinic's privacy policy.

## Data handling

This demonstration uses synthetic data only. No real patient information is stored. Phone numbers are masked in dashboards and logs, showing only the last four digits. The full number is never written to logs. Appointment records are stored in a local database and are used solely to demonstrate booking, rescheduling and cancellation workflows.

## Safety and scope

The voice assistant is an administrative receptionist. It does not provide diagnoses, medicine recommendations, dosages or treatment instructions. If you ask for medical advice, it will politely decline and offer an appointment. In an emergency, it will advise you to call 112 immediately and will offer a human handoff. You may request a human agent at any time.

## Accessibility

If you have difficulty hearing, you may use the live captions shown on screen. You can also type instead of speaking by using the text input. The interface is designed for keyboard use and works on a standard laptop.

## Example exchange 1
Caller: I would like to book an appointment. Assistant: Certainly. Which doctor would you like to see? Caller: Dr. Mehta, tomorrow morning. Assistant: Dr. Mehta has a slot at ten in the morning. Shall I book it? The assistant always reads back the exact details and waits for a clear yes before confirming. This prevents mistakes with dates, times and names.
