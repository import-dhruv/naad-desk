# Troubleshooting and Common Requests

## Audio problems

If the caller's voice is not being heard, check that the microphone is enabled in the browser and that the correct input device is selected. The interface shows a listening indicator when audio is flowing. If the connection is unstable, the gateway reconnects with short back-off delays. Audio is streamed continuously, including silence, so voice activity detection works correctly.

## Understanding errors

If the assistant repeats a question, it may not have understood a name or number. Spell names letter by letter and say phone numbers in clear groups. If the system cannot confirm a booking, it will not create one. You can always ask to speak with a human agent.

## Language switching

You may switch languages at any time by simply speaking in the new language. The assistant detects the change and re-pins the language for the next turn. If a switch is not recognised, repeat your sentence or use the language selector.

## Costs and limits

Calls are metered and a running cost estimate is shown on screen. A spend guard prevents new requests once the configured ceiling is reached. Calls are also limited in duration. These limits keep the demonstration safe and predictable.
